This policy explains which personal data CRUZMI SAS processes when you use the Cruzmi mobile app and the www.cruzmi.com website, why, for how long, who it is shared with, and how to exercise your rights.

## 1. Data controller

**CRUZMI SAS**, a French simplified joint-stock company with a share capital of €30,000, registered with the Créteil Trade and Companies Register under number 948 690 623, whose registered office is at 1 rue Jean-Moulin, 94130 Nogent-sur-Marne, France.

For any question about your personal data: [contact@cruzmi.com](mailto:contact@cruzmi.com).

## 2. Data processed, purposes and legal bases

### 2.1. Your account

- **Data**: email address, name or username, password (stored in encrypted form), profile photo and phone number if you choose to provide them.
- **Sign in with Apple or Google**: if you choose this sign-in method, we receive your email address and, where applicable, your name from that service.
- **Purpose**: create and manage your account, authenticate you, send you the emails needed for the service (account confirmation, password reset).
- **Legal basis**: performance of the contract (the [terms of use](/en/terms/)).

### 2.2. Your projects and their content

- **Data**: information about the projects you create or join (title, dates, destination, schedule, stops, accommodation, activities), expenses and budget, meals, shopping list, shared resources and items, dates of attendance, photos shared in the gallery.
- **Visibility**: this content is visible to the participants of the project concerned.
- **Purpose**: provide the app's features.
- **Legal basis**: performance of the contract.

### 2.3. Location

- **Data**: your device's position.
- **Use**: only if you allow it in your phone's settings, to show your position on the project map. Your position is neither stored on our servers nor shared with other participants.
- **Legal basis**: your consent, which you can withdraw at any time in your phone's settings.

### 2.4. Photo album orders

- **Data**: first name, last name, delivery address, phone number, email address, order details and tracking.
- **Payment**: handled by our payment provider Stripe. CRUZMI SAS never has access to your card details.
- **Purpose**: process, print, deliver and invoice your order, and provide after-sales service.
- **Legal basis**: performance of the contract; compliance with our legal (accounting and tax) obligations.

### 2.5. Website contact form

- **Data**: first name, last name, email address, subject and content of your message.
- **Purpose**: reply to your request.
- **Legal basis**: our legitimate interest in replying to people who contact us.

### 2.6. Technical data and security

- **Data**: IP address, technical logs, device type and system version.
- **Purpose**: ensure the operation and security of the service and prevent abuse (in particular the anti-bot protection of the contact form).
- **Legal basis**: our legitimate interest in securing the service.

We do not use your data for advertising and we do not sell it to anyone.

## 3. Recipients and processors

Your data is only accessible to authorised staff of CRUZMI SAS and, as far as they are concerned, to the other participants in your projects. We use the following providers, who act on our instructions:

| Provider | Role | Location |
|---|---|---|
| Contabo GmbH | Hosting of app data | Germany (EU) |
| Cloudflare, Inc. | Website hosting, anti-bot protection (Turnstile) | United States |
| Brevo (Sendinblue SAS) | Delivery of contact form messages | France (EU) |
| Stripe Payments Europe Ltd | Album payments | Ireland (EU) |
| Flexilivre | Album printing and delivery | France (EU) |
| Apple and Google | Sign in with Apple or Google (if you choose it), maps and place search | United States |

Your data may also be disclosed to the competent authorities where required by law.

## 4. Transfers outside the European Union

Some providers (Cloudflare, Apple, Google) are based in the United States. These transfers are governed by the EU–US Data Privacy Framework where the provider participates in it, or otherwise by the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

## 5. Retention periods

| Data | Period |
|---|---|
| Account and project content | As long as your account is active; deleted or anonymised after 2 years of inactivity |
| Albums generated in the app | 15 days |
| PDF file of a purchased album | 2 days after payment |
| Orders and invoices | 10 years (accounting obligation) |
| Contact form messages | As long as needed to handle your request, then 3 years at most |
| Technical logs | 12 months at most |

When you delete your account, your personal data is deleted, except data we must keep under a legal obligation. Your contributions to projects shared with other participants (expenses, photos…) remain visible to them in anonymised form.

## 6. Your rights

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the French Data Protection Act, you have the following rights over your data:

- right of access;
- right to rectification;
- right to erasure;
- right to restriction of processing;
- right to data portability;
- right to object, in particular to processing based on our legitimate interest;
- right to withdraw your consent at any time;
- right to set instructions regarding what happens to your data after your death.

You can delete your account directly in the app (Preferences → "Delete my Cruzmi account"). To exercise your other rights, write to [contact@cruzmi.com](mailto:contact@cruzmi.com). We may ask you to prove your identity.

If, after contacting us, you believe your rights have not been respected, you may lodge a complaint with the French data protection authority (CNIL): [www.cnil.fr](https://www.cnil.fr), or with the supervisory authority of your country of residence.

## 7. Minors

The app is intended for people aged 16 and over. Minors under 16 may only use it with the prior permission of the holder(s) of parental authority.

## 8. Cookies and trackers

The website www.cruzmi.com does not set **any advertising or audience-measurement cookies**, and therefore does not display a consent banner. Fonts are hosted on our own servers.

On the Contact page only, the Cloudflare Turnstile anti-bot service may process technical data from your browser to check that the form is being sent by a person. This processing, strictly necessary for the security of the form, is not used to track you.

## 9. Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data: encrypted connections (HTTPS), encrypted passwords, and access restricted to authorised staff only.

## 10. Changes

We may update this policy, in particular to reflect new features. The date of the last update is shown at the top of this page; in the event of a significant change, we will inform you in the app or by email.
